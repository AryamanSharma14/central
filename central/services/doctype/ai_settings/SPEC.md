# AI

## Purpose

Central sells AI served by Grove. A team with AI is one Central Team at Grove, named by the team id. Grove owns the models, the rate limits, the usage and the API keys, and each key carries its own policy: the geography it works in, the models it may call, its rate limits, and the cap it may spend each month. Central keeps no key: a key's secret is shown once, in the answer that mints it.

## Configuration

AI Settings holds the Grove URL and Central's control credential. A System Manager sets **Grove URL** and selects **Enroll** with the bootstrap secret Grove was given. Grove mints Central's control user and key (`grove.api.create_control_client`), and Central stores them. Enroll is a plain whitelisted call, not a form method, so the secret arrives at the top of the request and is popped before anything logs it. **Rotate Credential** asks Grove for a new secret under the current one (`grove.api.create_control_client_key`); the old one stops at once.

## Operation

- **Turning AI on**: `central.services.api.ai.enable_ai(team)` inserts a Team Service with `add_on_service = "ai"`. Its `before_insert` checks the team has no AI yet, then registers it at Grove by its id alone (`provision_team`; Grove keeps no email or contact) and sends its limits (`update_team`, below), so a refusal there leaves no row. `enable` locks the Team row, so two enables at once make one service. An `ai` row has no region, no subscription and no endpoint: each key calls its own geography's gateway. A team has one.
- **Keys**: `list_api_keys`, `create_api_key`, `set_api_key_caps` and `revoke_api_key` pass through to Grove with `team`. Only `create_api_key` returns a secret. A key is minted in a geography (one of `get_ai().geographies`; Grove's default when none is picked), with a monthly cap in USD; minted without one, it starts at 0 and its gateway refuses it until `set_api_key_caps` gives it one. The geography never changes: a team spans geographies by minting a key in each. `set_api_key_caps` sets several keys' caps in one call (the console's **Set monthly caps**, one row per live key); Grove saves lowers first and all or none, refusing caps that would add up past its tier's monthly spend limit, so the keys together never outspend the tier. A key at its cap is refused until the month ends or the cap is raised. Each key has its own rate limits (Grove's defaults: 20 requests and 100 000 tokens a minute) and the models it may call, decided at Grove. Grove refuses a key another team holds, refuses to revoke a key younger than six hours (it lists each key's `revocable_at`, and the console holds Revoke back until then rather than asking), and holds a team to its tier's key count.
- **Models**: `get_models(team, geography)` lists what a key in a geography starts with (the published models of Grove's default group there), for the overview and the keys tab's **How to call** (a geography picker, that geography's base URL and its models); `create_api_key` answers with what the new key may call.
- **Tier limits and standing**: each Trust Tier Level carries **Max AI Keys** and **Max AI Spend** (USD a month, whatever the team's currency). A team on **Manual Override** may carry its own **Override Max AI Spend** on its Billing Profile (blank keeps the level's). `services.ai.get_team_limits` reads them through `get_team_caps` with whether the team's anchor Subscription is Suspended, and `update_team` sends them to Grove: on enable, and from hooks when a Billing Profile's tier or override, a Subscription's standing or a level's AI fields change (`on_tier_update`, `on_level_update`, queued after commit). A suspended team's keys are all refused; recovery lifts it. An untiered team sends no key count and a spend limit of 0. A lowered limit stops no key: Grove refuses only new caps and raises until the team fits.
- **Spend, usage**: `get_ai` carries the team's spend from Grove (`grove.api.spend`) — this month's; `capped`, Σ the live caps, against `spend_limit`; `max_keys`; `suspended` — and this month's requests, tokens and cost, asked of Grove on every page open, uncached, so it matches the usage page. `get_usage` reads a period from Grove; usage of one key is narrowed by the key's hash, which Grove lists with it.
- **Billing and credit**: AI is postpaid and Grove holds no money. Credit is the team's Credit Wallet here, used before the card like for every other line. The month-end AI invoice line (the month's cost from `grove.api.usage`) is not wired into Central's invoicing yet.

`central.integrations.grove.GroveClient` is the only code that calls Grove.
